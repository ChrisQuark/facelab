A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Make yourself
comfortable.

Do I need to use all three services?+

Not at all. Nutrition, psychology and skin care are available independently. You can choose one service or explore additional support if it is useful to you.

What happens during a skin consultation?+

You can discuss your skin concerns and explore 3D skin analysis, treatment options and skincare products. Skin analysis is an assessment tool, not a substitute for a medical diagnosis. The therapist will discuss whether a treatment is suitable before you proceed.

Will my practitioners share information?+

Choosing one service does not automatically involve the others. Any coordination of your care should be discussed with you, with appropriate consent and respect for each professional’s confidentiality obligations.

Where is FaceLab located?+

FaceLab is in central Limassol, within the same clinic as the dental scan centre. The exact address, contact details and opening hours will be added before launch.
